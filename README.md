# JAN-SARTHI AI (जन-सारथी AI)

### Multilingual AI Citizen Feedback & Infrastructure Prioritization Platform
**Track 1 — AI for Digital Public Infrastructure & Governance (BRICS Theme: Innovation)**

*Tagline:* **"Every voice becomes evidence. Every evidence-backed need becomes visible."**

---

## 1. Problem Statement
Governments struggle to consolidate fragmented citizen feedback across diverse regional languages and connect it with national capital expenditure priorities. This leads to misaligned public spending, linguistic exclusion, and severe representation bias where affluent urban complaints overshadow critical rural deficits.

## 2. Solution Overview
JAN-SARTHI AI is an open-source Digital Public Good (DPG) that transforms unstructured multilingual citizen voice/text complaints into structured, geo-referenced, and evidence-fused infrastructure intelligence.

### Core Innovations:
1. **Zero-LLM Arithmetic Rule:** Pure Python deterministic computing for multi-criteria decision analysis (MCDA) and statistical aggregates.
2. **Demographic Representation-Bias Correction:** Inverse digital-literacy and connectivity weighting so marginalized rural/tribal communities receive equitable prioritization.
3. **Multi-Tier Security & Untrusted Boundary:** Pre-LLM prompt-injection defense, edge PII scrubbing (DPDP Act / LGPD compliance), anti-astroturfing velocity detection, and post-LLM numeric claim verification.
4. **Google ADK & Gemini Orchestration:** Google ADK coordinates tools; Google Gemini synthesizes evidence-grounded policy briefs.
5. **Human-in-the-Loop Sovereign Governance:** AI provides decision support; authorized officials verify evidence, challenge scores, and commit justifications to an immutable audit ledger.
